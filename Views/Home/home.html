Registry Overview

OSPBCR is a statewide system for understanding where, when, and among whom cancer occurs in Odisha. It brings information from healthcare facilities and reporting centres into a consistent population-based record.

From consistent reporting to a clearer picture of cancer

Through systematic case finding, validation, coding, de-duplication, and analysis, the registry builds reliable evidence that can be compared across locations and over time.

This shared evidence helps public-health leaders, clinicians, and researchers recognise patterns, identify information gaps, and plan responses that reflect the needs of Odisha's communities.

From Source to Statewide Insight

Operational Workflow

A traceable four-stage process moves cancer information from case identification to quality-assured analysis and reporting. Each stage has a distinct responsibility and a defined output for the next stage.

01

Data collection

Identify eligible cases and build a structured record from recognised reporting sources.

  • Prepare and maintain source lists
  • Retrieve cancer patient information from the source
  • Abstract essential details for cancer cases
  • Record source and case information consistently
Stage outputStructured case record ready for verification
→
02

Validation

Confirm that collected information is accurate, complete, consistent, and suitable for analysis.

  • Cross-verify cancer patient information
  • Perform completeness and data-quality checks
  • Resolve duplicate or conflicting entries
  • Complete quality control by a cancer registry expert
Stage outputQuality-assured registry dataset
→
03

Analysis

Convert verified records into population-level measures, comparisons, and interpretable patterns.

  • Conduct district-wise and block-wise data analysis
  • Calculate appropriate cancer registry rates
  • Compare patterns across place and time
  • Identify notable trends and information gaps
Stage outputEvidence on cancer burden, distribution, and trends
→
04

Reporting

Communicate findings in clear formats that support review, planning, and public-health action.

  • Report findings to the State of Odisha
  • Present cancer patterns at state and district levels
  • Share evidence for programme and service planning
  • Provide suggestions and recommendations to the Government of Odisha
Stage outputActionable summaries and recommendations
