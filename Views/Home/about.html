OUR PURPOSE

Objectives of the Odisha State Population-Based Cancer Registry

Building a complete and reliable picture of cancer across Odisha—so evidence can guide prevention, care, policy and public health investment.

STATEWIDE PUBLIC HEALTH MISSION
30 Districts
7 Regional areas

One connected population-based cancer registry for Odisha.

01

Statewide registry coverage

Establish Population-Based Cancer Registries in all 30 districts of Odisha to understand the true burden of cancer across the state.

Consistent population-level surveillance will include urban, rural and underserved communities. Comparable district records will reveal geographic patterns and guide focused action.

Every district One statewide picture
02

Reliable cancer evidence

Generate dependable district-level data on cancer incidence, mortality, prevalence and survival, analysed by sex, age group and cancer type.

Standardised collection and validation will make trends comparable across districts and over time. These insights will identify priority populations and help health teams measure outcomes.

  • Incidence
  • Mortality
  • Prevalence
  • Survival
03

National planning contribution

Contribute OSPBCR findings to national cancer registry data, supporting the development of informed cancer policies at the national level.

Sharing harmonised data will ensure Odisha's cancer patterns are represented in national estimates. State evidence can shape broader prevention, screening, treatment and research priorities.

Odisha districts National policy
04

Policy and programme impact

Aid cancer control policy and resource allocation by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Odisha, and strengthen ongoing cancer control programmes.

Reliable evidence will direct funding, workforce and services to areas of greatest need. Regular monitoring will show what works and where programmes require improvement.

Smarter allocation Stronger programmes
