01 Odisha State Population-Based Cancer Registry Portal
   Subtitle: A digital platform for cancer information, district intelligence, public resources and registry operations in Odisha.

02 Why a centralized portal is needed
   Trusted information; statewide visibility; digital access; better decisions.

03 How the portal is used
   Public/patients; researchers/clinicians; programme/CMS teams.

04 The advantages of one connected platform
   One source; faster access; transparent reach; operational control; digital record; professional and planning value.

05 Home
   Guided entry point to registry purpose, current updates and major public modules.

06 About OSPBCR
   Mandate, institutional partnerships, objectives, team/gallery/circular context.

07 Training & Training Materials
   District training activities, participant information, downloadable learning resources.

08 Interactive Map & District Coverage
   30 districts, facilities/sources, case status and district statistics.

09 Population Projection
   District/year population views for denominators, comparison and planning.

10 Cancer Burden & Registry Statistics
   Site/age incidence and mortality views backed by registry APIs.

11 Trending, News & Circulars
   Current institutional communication and localized public updates.

12 Reports, PDFs & Downloads
   Training PDFs, district cancer-burden resources and Odisha circulars.

13 CMS Administration
   Role-based publishing, localized content, managed uploads and operational control.

14 How the website and databases connect
   ASP.NET Core/public UI/API/CMS linked to Setu_Odisha SQL, OSPBCR_PORTAL SQL, JSON and upload storage.

15 A digital foundation for cancer intelligence
   Accessible by design; data with purpose; built to evolve.
